## What the site is

The Global Energy Map is a free, open-source research tool for exploring the world's oil and gas system: reserves, infrastructure, trade and LNG flows, and simple disruption scenarios. It is built from public, third-party datasets, each cited on the [Methodology](/methodology) and [Data](/data) pages.

## Not advice

The site is provided for research, teaching and general information. **Nothing on it is investment, trading, commercial, security, legal or policy advice**, and it should not be the sole basis for any decision.

The disruption scenarios are **first-order exposure accounting**: annual bilateral trade multiplied by fixed, hand-set route shares. They do not model prices, rerouting, stocks, substitution or timing. They are not forecasts of what would happen in a real disruption. The [Methodology](/methodology) page sets out the method and its limits.

## Accuracy and completeness

The data comes from third parties (Global Energy Monitor, NETL, the Energy Institute, CEPII BACI, LNG-T3, OpenStreetMap and others). It has known gaps, lags, duplicates and errors, some of which are documented on the Methodology page. We correct problems when we find them, but we do not guarantee that any figure, location, capacity, date or scenario result is accurate, complete or current. Check anything that matters against the original source.
